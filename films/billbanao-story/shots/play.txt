About this app
arrow_forward
Create professional quotations in less than a minute, manage customer leads, share invoices directly on WhatsApp, and convert quotations into GST-compliant invoices with a single click. BillBanao is built for Indian businesses that want to respond faster, look more professional, and close more deals without complicated software.

Designed for traders, retailers, wholesalers, distributors, manufacturers, contractors, agencies, freelancers, and service providers, BillBanao helps you manage the complete customer journey—from enquiry to quotation to invoice—all from your phone.

WHY BUSINESSES CHOOSE BILLBANAO

★ Create Quotations in Under a Minute

Prepare professional quotations quickly using saved products, GST rates, and customer details. Respond faster to enquiries and never keep customers waiting.

★ Lead Management Built In

Capture and organize customer enquiries in one place. Store customer information, manage leads, and keep track of potential business opportunities.

★ Share Invoices Directly on WhatsApp

Generate clean PDF quotations and invoices and send them instantly to customers through WhatsApp, email, or other apps.

★ One-Click Quotation to GST Invoice

When a quotation is approved, convert it into a GST-compliant invoice with a single tap. Customer details, products, discounts, and taxes are carried forward automatically.
