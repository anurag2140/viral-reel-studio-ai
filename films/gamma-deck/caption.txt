TITLE
AI Hack #1: Your boss thinks you worked all weekend on this deck

DESCRIPTION
Open Gamma, paste your notes, click Generate. You get a full deck with a design and pictures.
Try this prompt: "Make a 10-slide presentation from these notes. Clean, modern design with pictures. Notes: [paste your notes]"
Then click Export and download it as a PowerPoint file.
The free plan gives you 400 credits at signup and up to 10 slides per prompt, and export to PowerPoint is included.
