What is an ATS-friendly resume?

An ATS-friendly resume is a resume that Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) can scan and rank in their databases. Many companies use an ATS, especially those that deal with a high volume of applications. Its main goal is to sift through resumes and create a shortlist of qualified applicants.
